Growing @annacitrus

My story with content creation is a long one, I began creating Musicallys and unpublished YouTube videos at the age of eight. I loved making funny videos and unboxings. When I became a freshman in college, I kept seeing people online making videos, and I thought “I could get that many views too”, so I did it. I posted almost every day throughout my second semester of college, more for fun than to actually grow.

In October 2026, I decided it was time to actually go back and take content creation seriously. I decided on being firmly in the tech niche, with a focus on hardware. I am now experimenting with numerous different formats in trial Reels, and figuring out what is converting the most followers.

The current plan is to post videos focusing on virality (funny, relatable, and trendy content) in order to get views, videos focusing on education (how-tos, resources, tips) to get followers, and videos focusing on my projects and myself to gain fans.

The goal is to reach 100k by 2027.
