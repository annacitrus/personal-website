How I grew StarkHacks

We wanted this to be the guiness world record for the largest hardware hackathon, and it was our first year running it. This meant we needed to get in front of as many eyes as possible to get as many sign-ups as possible.

We were able to do this through two ways: Instagram Reels and email outreach to other college's professors and students. I mainly focused on the Instagram Reels.

We would post about 5 videos per week. I would come up with concepts (usually based off other viral videos I have seen on my feed), then the executive board would meet on Sundays to film. I would act in and direct these videos. Then, our editor would edit and the president would post the videos. It was a well-oiled machine.
